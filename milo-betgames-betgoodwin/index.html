The challenge

More launches. More to check. No new hires.

Every launch added sessions, currencies, regions and partners for a lean team to watch. Two teams felt it first.

Risk & Integrity

Led by Aivaras Juškevičius, Head of Risk & Integrity

  1. 01Analysts stitched together queries, spreadsheets and several back-office tools by hand.
  2. 02Partner reports were compiled as PDFs, one at a time.
  3. 03There was no fast way to explain a risk spike: velocity, bots, syndicates, geo mismatch or play pattern.
  4. 04Hours could pass between detection and action, and CRM notes were done later or forgotten.

Getting started

One desk first. Then the others.

No engineering sprint and no long implementation. We do the building.

STEP 1

Connect

Start with one desk on read-only access. AML is the closest match to BetGames, so it is a natural first choice.

STEP 2

Define

We teach Milo your terms and thresholds: net deposits, gross win, BOG, your enhanced checks levels and complaint stages.

STEP 3

Set access

AML reasons stay with AML. Customer Service sees what it can tell a customer. Operations sees the trading and payments view.

STEP 4

Ask

Each desk asks in the browser, Slack, Teams or WhatsApp. The other desks join the same workspace when they're ready.

Built for a regulated desk.

Milo is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Connections are read-only, access is set by role, and every question is logged with who asked it and what Milo returned.

When Milo takes an action, it runs with the controls BetGames uses: approvals above a threshold, write-backs limited to allowed fields, an undo, and an audit trail nobody can edit. Where data has to stay on your own servers, Milo can run there.
