Dear Luke and Helen,

Good to meet you both at the show. Since then I've read up on AFB: founded in 1918, four generations on, 20-plus brands of your own, partner brands with Rochelle Humes and Leo Bancroft, and ranges in most of the UK's big retailers.

Taking a product from idea to shelf leaves data everywhere. Factory orders and test results, containers and the warehouse, each retailer's sales portal, royalties and every brand's social channels. One question across them usually means exports and a spreadsheet.

Milo puts it in one place that anyone at AFB can ask in plain English, from the account managers to the design studio. Kellogg's uses it across dozens of markets, and Aspecta and Baker & Baker have just joined. Everything below runs on sample data, so click around.

Liam James Rabone

Co-Founder, Milo · liam@milo.ai

    Kellogg's · Commercial finance

    Sales fell across the Middle East. The cause was on the shelf.

    Kellogg's commercial finance team uses Milo across dozens of markets, tracking trade spend, promotional ROI and margin across hundreds of SKUs. When sales fell across the Middle East and North Africa, Milo traced the fall to a shortage of Coco Pops rather than a drop in demand.

    AFB sells eight categories through many retailers. A dip at one of them is a stock question as often as a demand one. Try the Accounts tab above.

    Getting started

    Start with one team.

    A pilot starts with one agile team, say the account managers, where the retailer data lives. The rest of AFB joins in waves.

    STEP 1

    Connect

    Retailer portals, accounts, the warehouse and shipping, read-only and on a sandbox first. Our engineers build it and onboard the team in person.

    STEP 2

    Teach

    We teach Milo your terms: brands and categories, critical paths, rate of sale, weeks of cover and how each retailer funds a promotion.

    STEP 3

    Ask

    The team asks in Teams, Slack or the browser, and pins what matters as dashboards and alerts.

    STEP 4

    Add teams

    Brand, sourcing and QA, logistics and finance join in waves. Users are unlimited, so everyone can have a login.
