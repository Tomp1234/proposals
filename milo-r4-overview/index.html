07 // Getting started

Run it like a campaign. Approved first.

The same order R4 runs media in. Start with the ledger, HubSpot and the booking and delivery files, and the next board pack could be the first one Milo builds.

STEP 1

Approve

Your team signs off read-only access: the ledger and HubSpot first, then bookings, delivery reports and timesheets.

STEP 2

Activate

We build the integrations and teach Milo R4's terms: approved position, VBD, make-good, post-flight, as you define them.

STEP 3

Deliver

Leadership, operations, finance and new business ask from the day the connections are live. Each person sees what their role allows.

STEP 4

Verify

The questions that matter keep running: Monday's control loop, each post-flight report, the monthly board pack.
