The challenge

Four businesses in one company, and no way to see across them.

Wholesale ran on Breww. The taproom till ran on Toast. Accounting ran on two Xero accounts. People sat in BrightHR, rep activity in Bowimi, documents in SharePoint. Every question that crossed a boundary — and most of the important ones did — got answered by hand or not at all.

What we built

Twenty dashboards and twenty apps, one department at a time.

Wholesale

Weekly sales by hectolitre volume and net revenue — current, month-to-date and year-to-date — plus top ten customers, a new business tracker by rep, and a churn-risk board that flags customers who have gone quiet.
