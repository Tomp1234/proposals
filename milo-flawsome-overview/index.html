Getting started

Built for a team your size.

No data hire and no engineering sprint. We do the building, so nobody at Flawsome! has to become an analyst.

STEP 1

Connect

Commercial first: Shopify, the Wonky Club, wholesale orders and distributor reports, on read-only access. Stock, production and finance join next.

STEP 2

Define

We teach Milo your terms: cases and units, what counts as an active site, the fruit in each product, and one exchange rate for the UAE.

STEP 3

Set access

National accounts see their accounts. Operations sees stock and production. Margins stay with whoever you choose.

STEP 4

Ask

The team asks in the browser, email, Slack, Teams or WhatsApp, and the monthly packs arrive on their own.

Each account sees only its own.

Milo is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Connections are read-only, access is set by role, and every question is logged with who asked it and what Milo returned.

An account review or impact report only ever shows one account's numbers. What one customer pays never appears in another's pack, and margins stay with the people you choose.
