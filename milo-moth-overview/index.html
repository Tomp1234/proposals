Getting started

Connect it before the Christmas peak.

December is the busiest month for gifting, parties and corner shops. Connect this year's data now, and the Christmas readout is a question rather than a project.

STEP 1

Connect

Start with one grocer, the wholesaler depletions, Shopify and the ledger, on read-only access. Last year's data works fine.

STEP 2

Define

We teach Milo MOTH's terms: rate of sale, distribution points, depletions, singles and four-packs, trade spend, batch spec.

STEP 3

Set access

A rep sees their own patch, a US distributor its own states, a manager their team, and finance everything.

STEP 4

Ask, then act

Answers start the day the connections are live, in Slack, Teams, WhatsApp or the browser.
