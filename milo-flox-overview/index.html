    Getting started

    Start with one team.

    A pilot starts with one agile team, say marketplace operations, then the rest of FLOX joins in waves.

    STEP 1

    Connect

    A read-only login on a replica of the platform database, plus HubSpot, on a sandbox first. Our engineers build it and onboard the team in person.

    STEP 2

    Teach

    We teach Milo your terms: requests, quotes, bookings, match rate, commission, on time in full and despatch adherence.

    STEP 3

    Ask

    The team asks in Slack, Teams or the browser, and pins what matters as dashboards and alerts.

    STEP 4

    Add teams

    Network, growth, finance and leadership join in waves. Users are unlimited, so everyone can have a login.
