Data and security

Clients who say no to AI stay out.

Some of your clients don't want AI near their data at all. The rule sits in your database's own security model, so Milo never reads them.

Column rule · member detailshidden for every client

  • Excluded at the source

    Row and column-level security hooks into your database's own permissions, so excluded clients and columns never leave it.

  • Processed in Frankfurt

    All processing runs in a Frankfurt data centre, covering the EU and the UK.

  • Not kept, not trained on

    Under our enterprise API agreement, your data isn't retained or used for training, and that's legally protected.

  • Wiped when you leave

    Close the account and all of your data is deleted.
