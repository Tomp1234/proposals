Dear Sophie,

Congratulations on the CFO role. I met Áron Farkas at the Ticketing Business Forum in Manchester earlier this year, and he thought Milo would be useful for TicketSwap. The word from the data team was to pick it up once the ClickHouse migration was done, and Áron has since told me it is.

A resale marketplace is hard to read from one system. The fee sits in ClickHouse, the payment in Stripe, payouts wait on the event, chargebacks arrive weeks later, and the ledger comes last. One clean number per market usually means someone in finance waiting on someone in data. TicketSwap's August ad for a Senior Financial Accountant talked about scaling finance through “automation, AI and better tooling”. This page shows what that could look like.

Milo connects to the systems you already run, ClickHouse included. Anyone in the company asks in plain English and gets the answer in seconds, with its sources, and any answer can become a dashboard or a scheduled report. The reporting that sits in a queue gets built by the people who need it.

Ticketing is where we started. Our first proof of concept was See Tickets, and I came into this from the industry myself, at TicketArena, The Warehouse Project and Ibiza Rocks. We have terms out with two ticketing companies and none in resale, so TicketSwap would be the first.

Getting started

Safe, easy, fair. Then fast.

TicketSwap's own three promises, in the order we'd connect. Start with ClickHouse, Stripe and the ledger in the next few weeks, and November's close could be the first one Milo helps with.

STEP 1

Safe

Read-only access to ClickHouse first, then Stripe, the ledger and the bank. Your team signs off every connection.

STEP 2

Easy

We build the integrations and teach Milo TicketSwap's terms: GMV, take rate, net revenue, markets and partner events, as the data team defines them.

STEP 3

Fair

Finance sees the money, each market team its own market, and an organiser only its own events.

STEP 4

Fast

Answers start the day the connections are live. The questions that matter keep running, in Slack, Teams, email or the browser.
