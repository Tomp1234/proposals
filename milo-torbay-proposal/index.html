What Milo does and doesn’t do under your QMS

Read-only

Milo reads and reports. It never changes source data, so your systems of record stay as they are.

Not a validated MES

Milo is for analysis and decision support. It doesn’t replace a validated pharma MES, and it doesn’t make release decisions.

Scheduling is a test

Finite scheduling is possible if the data is connected. In this month we test whether your data can support it. We aren’t promising it as a deliverable.
