Dear Dani,

Doll Beauty started in 2016 with lashes, because you and Sam saw a gap for an affordable, high-quality one. Ten years on, dollbeauty.com carries 54 products, from the Danielle lash to 17 nudes and the Solid Start brushes that sold out and came back.

Each of those products now sells in several places at once: your own shop, the retailers, creator codes and the inbox. A simple question, like how many weeks of Gripping Primer are left, has its answer spread across Shopify, the warehouse, retailer orders and next week's email. Usually someone joins that up by hand.

Milo connects it all. Anyone at Doll asks in plain English and gets the answer in seconds, with its sources. A question can keep running, so the Monday restock list and the month's figures build themselves.
