How I got there
  • Trip value is the ticket plus every add-on (river cruise, walking tours, attractions), less third-party commission and refunds, divided by the passengers on the booking.
  • Where the add-on rate is low, most tickets come through third parties who don't sell the extras. In Rome, 73% of passengers booked elsewhere.
  • A passenger means a scan on board in Ventrata, the same in every city. That definition was agreed once and every report uses it.

03 · For your tech team

Light on Ventrata.
Careful with your data.

The booking engine

No queries on every click

Milo collects new and changed bookings on a schedule your tech team sets, away from peak hours. Dashboards, filters and apps run on Milo's own copy, so ten people opening the same report send nothing to Ventrata.

Your data

Read-only, in the EU

Hosted in Frankfurt. Milo never writes to Ventrata or anything else, and never trains on your data. Customer names can be left out: trip value needs bookings, not people. ISO 27001 and SOC 2, documents at trust.milo.ai.

The numbers

Same question, same answer

The arithmetic is never done by the language model. Milo writes a query, runs it on your data and shows its working, so every figure traces back to the booking. If your systems change, the engineer reconnects them.
