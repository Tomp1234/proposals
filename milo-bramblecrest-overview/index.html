    Getting started

    Start with one team.

    A pilot starts with one agile team, say the people who plan the season, then the rest of Bramblecrest joins in waves.

    STEP 1

    Connect

    Accounts, the trade and online shops, the warehouse and shipping, read-only and on a sandbox first. Our engineers build it and onboard the team in person.

    STEP 2

    Teach

    We teach Milo your terms: ranges and seasons, landed cost, weeks of cover and what counts as on time.

    STEP 3

    Ask

    The team asks in Teams, Slack or the browser, and pins what matters as dashboards and alerts.

    STEP 4

    Add teams

    Sales, trade, service and finance join in waves. Users are unlimited, so every owner can have a login.
