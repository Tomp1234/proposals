Getting started

One market first. Then the rest.

No engineering sprint and no long implementation. We do the building.

STEP 1

Connect

Start with one market: retailer data, warehouse stock and shipments, all on read-only access.

STEP 2

Define

We teach Milo your terms: product names in each market, cases and packs, retailer codes, rupees, dirham and pounds.

STEP 3

Set access

Sales sees retailers, each agency sees its own campaigns, leadership sees every market.

STEP 4

Ask

The team asks in the browser, email, Teams or WhatsApp. Other markets join the same workspace, America included.

Your numbers, your rules.

Milo is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Connections are read-only, access is set by role, and every question is logged with who asked it and what Milo returned.

Agencies and distributors can have their own view without seeing anything else. Where data has to stay in a particular country, Milo can run on your own servers.
