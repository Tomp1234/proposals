09 Security

The detail IT will ask for.

From the Milo Trust Centre, where the full reports and policies are available on request.

Certified

ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type I. SOC 2 Type II is in progress. GDPR.

Hosted

AWS in Frankfurt, across several availability zones, with database backups. Or on your own servers.

Encrypted

In transit and at rest, with managed production keys.

Tested

Penetration tests, code scanning, a web application firewall and intrusion detection.

Separated

Each customer's data is kept apart, and our access to production is least-privilege.

Subprocessors

Amazon Web Services, ClickHouse, Fivetran, Cloudflare and Clerk.

On request: our SOC 2 Type I report, ISO 27001 certificate, and incident response, vendor management and network security policies, plus 23 more documents.

Questions IT usually asks

Does Milo change anything in our systems?

No. Every connection is read-only, and nothing is written back.

Can we switch it off?

Yes. Revoke the read-only logins and the feeds stop. We delete your data under our retention and deletion policy.

Who owns the data?

You do. The warehouse is yours, with full access, and Power BI can read it too.

Do we have to replace anything?

No. Milo reads the systems you already run.

Who keeps the connections working?

We do. When a system changes, our engineers fix the connection.
