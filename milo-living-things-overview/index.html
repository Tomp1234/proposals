Getting started

Built for a team your size.

No data hire and no engineering sprint. We build the connections, so nobody at Living Things has to become an analyst.

STEP 1

Connect

Retailer EPOS portals, wholesaler reports, Circana and Shopify first, on read-only access. Stock, finance and international follow.

STEP 2

Define

We teach Milo your terms: cans, four-packs and cases, what counts as a ranged store, the campaign neighbourhoods, and one exchange rate for euros and francs.

STEP 3

Set access

Sales sees volumes and rate of sale. Prices, terms and margins stay with whoever you choose.

STEP 4

Ask

The team asks in Slack, Teams, email, WhatsApp or the browser. Buyer packs, the campaign readout and the board pack arrive on their own.

Each retailer sees only its own.

Milo is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Connections are read-only, access is set by role, and every question is logged with who asked it and what Milo returned.

A buyer pack only ever shows one retailer's numbers. What one retailer pays never appears in another's pack, and margins stay with the people you choose.
