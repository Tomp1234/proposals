Milo and Copilot

You already have Copilot. Here's the difference.

Friends House runs on Microsoft, so this came up on the demo. The two do different jobs, and Milo answers in Teams alongside it.

Copilot is built for

  • Documents, email, meetings and chats in Microsoft 365
  • Looking across what it can find at the moment you ask
  • Drafting, summarising and rewriting
  • One person's question, answered in that conversation

Milo is built for

  • The numbers in every system, inside Microsoft and out: Rendezvous, Business Central, Mailchimp, Hootsuite and the tills
  • Bringing each system's data in and keeping its history, so this year compares with last
  • Learning how Friends House measures things: a delegate, a confirmed booking, a busy day
  • Forecasts, dashboards and alerts that keep running after the question
  • Connections built and maintained by our engineers

Use bothCopilot for the words. Milo for the numbers.

Proof

Urban Leisure Group · London bars and restaurants

Five systems. One Monday pack.

Toast, Harri, Marketman, Design My Night and QuickBooks each held a piece of the week. Every Monday someone logged into all five and rebuilt the numbers by hand. All five were connected in under two weeks, with no data team and no warehouse project.

Now the scorecard lands before anyone logs in. The revenue forecast adds events already booked in Design My Night to past trading, day by day, and usage grew tenfold month on month as the team found more to ask.

Why it's here. That forecast is the Friends House one: past trading plus what's already in the diary, read from Rendezvous instead of Design My Night.
