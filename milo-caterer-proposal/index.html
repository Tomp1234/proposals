    05 One source of truth

    Same question. Same answer.

    AI is now part of everyone's job. When each person asks on their own, the numbers drift in three ways.

    Silent misalignment

    Two definitions, and nobody notices

    Two reps ask how many clients are active and get different numbers. Each chat guessed what active means.

    Definitions are agreed once with your team and used in every answer.

    Context degradation

    Long chats drift

    A long chat loses track of the export pasted in at the start, and the figures slide with it.

    Every question goes back to the live data in Salesforce.

    Compliance bias

    Leading questions get a yes

    Ask whether the pipeline is up and a chatbot leans towards agreeing with you.

    Answers come from the data, with the sources under every one.

    Keep Claude for writing and thinking. Use Milo when the answer is a number.
