What Milo does

Ask it. Pin it. Let it run.

Three things, in this order. Nobody needs a data team for any of them.

Ask

Plain English in, answer out

Typed the way you'd ask a colleague. The answer comes back in seconds with its sources, so it can be checked rather than taken on trust.

Pin

Dashboards built by anyone

Pin an answer and it becomes a live dashboard that keeps itself up to date. Whoever asked the question builds it, without a ticket or a developer.

Automate

A question becomes a routine

Schedule it and the monthly report sends itself. Set a line and Milo says when a number crosses it, by email, Teams, Slack or WhatsApp.
