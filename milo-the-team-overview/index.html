Getting started

One department first. Then the rest.

No engineering sprint and no long implementation. We do the building.

STEP 1

Connect

Start with one department, such as Rights Sales or Finance, on read-only access to the systems it already uses.

STEP 2

Define

We teach Milo your terms: gross deal value, commission, properties and categories, regions, and every currency converted the same way.

STEP 3

Set access

Agents see their own clients, Finance sees every division, and leadership sees all of it.

STEP 4

Ask

The team asks in the browser, email, Slack or Teams. Each department joins the same workspace when it's ready.

Client terms stay with the right people.

Milo is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Connections are read-only, access is set by role, and every question is logged with who asked it and what Milo returned.

An agent sees their own clients' deals and nobody else's. Where data has to stay in a particular country, Milo can run on your own servers.
