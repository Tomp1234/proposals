Milo vs. Claude + Insights MCP, and Hex.

Security already built in

Part 2

Milo vs. Hex

The framing

Hex is built for the data team.
Milo is built for everyone else.

Hex would make the data team faster. Milo means business teams don't have to wait for the data team.

Hex

A workspace where analysts write SQL and Python in notebooks, then publish apps and dashboards. Its AI features (the Hex agent, Threads, and a governed data layer called Context Studio) sit on top of that. It still assumes a data team builds and looks after things.

Milo

Works like an AI analyst for the business team. People ask in plain English and get dashboards, reports, files and actions without the data team in the middle.
