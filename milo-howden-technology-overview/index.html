Does our data train a model?

No. It is used to answer your questions and nothing else.

Can Milo change our records?

No. Connections are read-only. Milo answers questions; it doesn't edit systems.

What happens when a system is upgraded?

Connections are watched for schema changes, and our engineers fix any break, usually within days.

How would we start?

With one firm, in a sandbox, on test data. Production comes later, and only when you say so.
