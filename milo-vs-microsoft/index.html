              Questions teams ask us.

              We already pay for Microsoft 365. Why not just use Copilot?

              Copilot is great for email, documents and meetings. For numbers, it answers from Power BI semantic models that someone has to build, keep up to date and prepare for AI. Milo comes with that work already done: connectors, shared definitions, permissions and alerts, kept running by our engineers.

              We’re moving to Fabric. Does Milo replace it?

              It doesn’t have to. Fabric is one of Milo’s connectors, so Milo can answer from what’s already in Fabric, plus the sources that aren’t there yet. If you’re not building a data platform, Milo gets you the answers without one.

              Is my data safe?

              Milo follows the permissions you set, so each person only sees the data they’re allowed to. Milo is built to ISO 27001, SOC 2 and GDPR standards, and we never train on your data.

              Do I need to know SQL or DAX?

              No. Ask in plain English. Milo writes the query, checks it and shows its working.

              Can I use both?

              Yes, and plenty of teams do. Copilot for documents and email, Milo for the numbers.
