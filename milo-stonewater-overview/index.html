      Trust

      Trust first. Then answers.

      • Data quality is a question you can ask

        Find where two systems disagree about a home, and send each mismatch to its owner before anyone relies on it.

      • A colleague decides

        Milo reads and suggests. It can’t change a record, and every answer shows where it came from.

      • Some records stay out

        Stonewater chooses what Milo can’t read. Refuge and Safe Space records never need to be connected.

      • Champions can join freely

        Users are unlimited. Start with one team or one locality, and let the people who want it bring the next.

        What’s landing

        Four changes. Each one is a question.

        1. October 2026STAIRs: publishing

          Landlords publish and maintain a scheme of information for tenants.

          What have we published that’s now out of date?

        2. 30 November 2026Awaab’s Law, Phase 2

          Adds excess cold and heat, falls, structural collapse, fire, electrical, hygiene and food safety hazards.

          Which open hazards are near a deadline, and who lives there?

        3. April 2027STAIRs: requests

          Tenants can ask for information. Stonewater’s data subject requests alone went from 162 to 289 a year in two years.

          Gather what we hold on this repair, ready to check.

        4. April 2027Rent convergence

          A more consistent approach to rent setting, which will be felt household by household.

          Who might need support before their rent changes?
