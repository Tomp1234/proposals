05 · Security

Your data stays yours, and stays where it is.

Six things an IT team asks before anything connects.

Access

Read-only, per person

Milo reads Fabric and never writes to it. Row and column permissions are enforced at the data layer, so a question outside someone's access returns nothing.

Audit

Every question logged

Every question, answer, share and action is logged with who did it. Single sign-on and multi-factor authentication as standard. The sanctioned alternative to a personal AI account.

Residency

UK and EU hosting

Data stays in region and goes nowhere you have not agreed to. Your own tenant is an option if policy prefers it.

Training

Never trained on

Your data is not used to train models, by us or by the model providers, with zero retention. It is written into the contract.

Models

The best model for each task

Each task goes to the best current model, Claude and Gemini among them. Inference runs on our servers, not the open internet.

Certification

ISO 27001 and SOC 2

GDPR, ICO registered and independently pen tested. The certificate and reports go to your IT team before anything connects, and sit at trust.milo.ai.
