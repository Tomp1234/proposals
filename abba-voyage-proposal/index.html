On dashboard fatigue

Nobody in the business wants another dashboard.

Fair. A dashboard answers the question somebody had when they built it, and every question after that comes back to you. Milo is the other way round: the question comes first, and a dashboard is only what you keep if the answer turns out to be worth watching.

01

Answers, in seconds, with the reason

Not a chart to interpret. A number, the movement behind it, and a short written read of what drove it, with the sources it used listed underneath.

02

Dashboards anyone can build

If a question gets asked twice, it becomes a view. Built by the person who asked it, in the browser, without a ticket to you and without a Power BI seat.

03

Automations that come out of the questions

A question asked once becomes a Monday morning email or an alert when a number moves. The reporting layer builds itself out of what people actually ask.
