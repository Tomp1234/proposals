Dear Rick,

Marussia is at the start of its AI journey, which is a good place to be. The companies that get furthest don't start with a chatbot. They start by getting their own numbers into one place, because an assistant that can't see a depletion can't tell you where a brand is working.

For a global brand team that's harder than it sounds. Your brands sell through fourteen distribution companies in twelve countries, each closing its own books, plus travel retail and partners beyond. The group sees what each market ordered. What sold through, and what the A&P bought, arrives later and in a different shape from every market.

Milo connects them. Our engineers build the connections, then anyone at Marussia asks a question in plain English and gets the answer in seconds, with its sources. Any answer can become a dashboard, a monthly pack or an alert, without a data project.

Below you'll find the journey in four steps, a demo that starts at your seat, and three drinks businesses that started where Marussia is now: Two Tribes, Prime Time and Dhillon's. Everything runs on sample data, so click around.

Three drinks businesses that started where Marussia is

Two Tribes Brewing · London · Case study

A drinks business with no data team.

Wholesale, 831 Tesco stores, a King's Cross taproom and a distribution arm, run by 35 people on seven systems that had never spoken to each other. No analysts and no implementation project.

Breww, two Xero accounts, Toast, BrightHR, Bowimi and SharePoint were connected in two weeks. The team has built twenty live dashboards of its own. Read the case study

Why it's here. The same mix of wholesale, grocery and on-trade that Marussia UK sells through, and the proof that a drinks business doesn't need a data team to start.

Prime Time · London · With Milo since September 2026

Where is our stock, across every retailer?

Founded in 2021 by Harvey Armstrong and Sam Holmes, Prime Time makes a lower-calorie lager sold through Waitrose, Little Waitrose at Shell and 144 Tesco Express stores in London, plus bars and events.

Milo connects Breww, HubSpot and Xero, and joins them to their retail, direct-to-consumer and marketing data. The question that mattered most was where their stock sat across all of their retailers, in one answer instead of a round of emails and spreadsheets.

Why it's here. Every Marussia brand lives on someone else's shelf or back bar. Knowing where it sits, and whether it's moving, is the first question for a brand owner too.

  1. StartedStock spread across other people's fridges
  2. ConnectedBreww, HubSpot and Xero
  3. JoinedRetail, direct-to-consumer and marketing data
  4. First questionWhere is our stock, retailer by retailer?

Dhillon's Brewery · Coventry · With Milo since September 2026

A brewery that's also its own customer.

Founded by Dal Dhillon in 2015, Dhillon's brews Ghost Town Lager in Coventry and runs its own bars, including the Sky Blue Tavern, the Spire Bar and the Dhillon's Lounge at the Coventry Building Society Arena.

Each venue runs its own till, booking and analytics accounts, so nobody saw the whole business at once. Milo joins them, with the CRM and the accounts, into one view, then puts stock and events on top.

Why it's here. Fourteen distribution companies each closing their own books is the same problem at a larger scale: many sets of numbers, one business, and nobody seeing all of it at once.

  1. StartedSeparate accounts for every venue
  2. ConnectedTills, bookings, CRM and accounts
  3. JoinedStock and events, venue by venue
  4. NowOne view of every bar
