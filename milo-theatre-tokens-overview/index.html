Why the CRM go-live makes this the right moment

A CRM tells you who your customers are. Your gift card platform tells you what was bought and redeemed. Finance tells you what it's worth. Right now those three stories live in three places, and joining them up means someone exporting spreadsheets.

  • The customer picture only completes when the CRM meets the redemption data. Who bought, what they bought, whether it was ever used, and at which theatre, all in one place.
  • New systems are when the questions start. "Is the CRM data clean?" "Did that campaign actually sell tokens?" Milo answers those on day one, instead of you waiting for reporting to be built.
  • You build on the CRM rather than around it. No second warehouse project, no BI rollout, no extra licences per person.
