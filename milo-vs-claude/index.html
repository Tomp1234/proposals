            Questions teams ask us.

            Can’t I just connect my data to Claude?

            You can connect some tools, but someone has to set it up, maintain it and decide what the numbers mean. Milo comes with that already handled: connectors, shared definitions, permissions and dashboards, built for business teams.

            Is Milo built on Claude?

            Milo uses a combination of leading models, including open source ones, tuned for working with business data.

            Is my data safe?

            Milo follows the permissions you set, so each person only sees the data they’re allowed to. Milo is built to ISO 27001, SOC 2 and GDPR standards.

            Do I need to know SQL?

            No. Ask in plain English. Milo writes the query, checks it and shows its working.

            Can I use both?

            Yes, and plenty of teams do. Claude for thinking, Milo for the numbers.
