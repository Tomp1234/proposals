The process

How it works.

Andy, your account manager, does this with your team. Most connectors are already built; anything new takes an hour or two.

01

Connect your data

OpenTable, Collins, Zonal, Actio and HubSpot plug in over API. Data syncs live, as often as every five minutes, hourly by default.

02

Clean and label

We sit with your team and agree what each field actually means across the three systems, so a cover is a cover everywhere.

03

Permissions and access

Role-based from day one. A BDM sees their dashboards and pipeline; finance data stays with finance. Unlimited seats either way.

04

Knowledge and rules

Your definitions and thresholds, such as petty cash tolerances and when a provisional becomes confirmed, written in, so answers match how ETM reports.

05

Rebuild the Monday pack

The weekly workbook becomes live dashboards, per venue and group-wide, run in parallel against twelve months of history to prove parity.

06

Milo works for you

Scheduled reports go on their own. A Friday noon summary to Ed and Tom by email or WhatsApp, daily numbers to GMs. Nobody rebuilds anything.
