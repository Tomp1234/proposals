    A1 · Architecture

    Everything inside Samsung's perimeter. Nothing outside it.

    The application is deployed in its entirety within Samsung's private cloud. AWS, GCP and Azure are all routine targets, and Samsung's own cloud platform is supported. There is no connection to Milo's servers, no telemetry, no third-party services in the serving path, and no data egress of any kind. The deployment is containerised and delivered with infrastructure-as-code, so Samsung's team can inspect, reproduce and audit the entire footprint.

    The Engine also operates headless: the same retrieval, analysis and generation services are exposed over an internal API, so the Engine can power other front-ends or sit inside a broader Samsung architecture if one emerges later.

    A2 · AI models

    Model-agnostic by design. The LLM is not the analyst.

    The deployment supports Samsung-selected language models, including internally hosted models such as Gauss, and commercial models deployed within Samsung's own cloud tenancy. Model choice is a configuration decision Samsung controls, and it can change over the life of the deployment without rework.

    The critical design decision is what the LLM is not asked to do. Language models are poor arithmetic and statistics engines, and we do not use them as one. All quantitative work, including shares, growth rates, windowed averages, significance tests, correlation, regression and time-series forecasting, is executed by dedicated statistical and modelling components. The LLM's role is language: understanding the question, orchestrating retrieval and analysis, and writing the grounded commentary on results it did not itself compute. This is the direct answer to the reasonable objection that LLMs cannot do real data analysis. They cannot, and in this architecture they are never asked to.

    A3 · Security and permissions model

    Inherited, not reinvented.

    • Identity: SSO against Samsung's identity provider. No separate credentials, no shadow user store.
    • Authorisation: role-based access control mapped to Samsung's existing confidentiality levels, data ownership and user role restrictions, as the brief requires. Row-level security applies to structured data, so two users asking the same question see only what each is entitled to see.
    • Permission-tagged retrieval: access rules are attached to content at ingestion and enforced at query time, before generation. An answer cannot cite a source its reader is not permitted to open.
    • Fenced categories: technical and product IP, and personnel and people data, are excluded architecturally. No connector to those systems exists in the deployment, no such content is indexed, and no configuration change inside the application can widen the fence.
    • Audit: queries, sources consulted and outputs are logged within Samsung's environment for review.
    • Practice: our engineering and operations follow SOC 2 and ISO 27001 aligned controls, and we operate for clients in regulated industries, including licensed gaming. Under full self-hosting, however, the certifications are largely moot: Samsung's existing cloud controls govern the running system, and no Milo personnel can reach Samsung data.
    A4 · RAG, grounding, citations and hallucination

    Provenance by design, not by promise.

    Unstructured content follows an ingestion, enrichment, vectorisation and retrieval pipeline; structured data is queried directly. At answer time the Engine retrieves candidate sources under the user's permissions, and generation is constrained to retrieved material. Every answer carries its citations: document title, page number where relevant, and click-back to the source. Confidence indicators are computed from source agreement, source recency and retrieval strength, and are shown with the answer rather than hidden in a log.

    Where sources are missing, the Engine says "I don't know" instead of improvising. Where sources are incomplete, outdated or contradictory, it says that too, identifies the gap, and states what additional evidence or human review is required, exactly as the brief specifies. Hallucination is addressed structurally rather than rhetorically: unsupported generation is excluded by the grounding constraint, quantitative claims come from the analysis engines rather than the language model, and the citation requirement makes every claim checkable by its reader. The user feedback facility closes the loop, routing weak answers into tuning.

    This appendix intentionally describes the pipeline at architecture level rather than component level. We are happy to go deeper under NDA in a technical session.

    A5 · Data ingestion: structured and unstructured

    Built for exactly the estate the brief describes.

    • Unstructured: PowerPoint, PDF, Word and Excel, at the Insights Bank's scale of up to 1,000 mixed files and well beyond. Slide structure, tables and embedded objects are parsed; charts that exist only as flat images are read by vision-model extraction, which recovers the underlying series and labels so the data inside them becomes citable and analysable. Extraction quality is validated on Samsung's real decks during weeks one to four, and low-confidence extractions are flagged, not asserted.
    • Automatic ingestion: the Engine watches the SMC location and ingests new and updated files as they arrive, with no manual step. This auto-refresh pattern is already running in our Kellanova deployment.
    • Structured: warehouse tables, Qlik Sense data, and internal APIs connect through standard connectors. The Qlik scope in the brief (market shares, market values, brand awareness, consideration and purchase intent, social metrics, sell-in and sell-out) is read directly, and Phase 3 adds automated ingestion of further internal and external datasets.
