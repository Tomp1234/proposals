    Trust & Compliance

    Security, privacy and data protection overview

    Milo.ai · Autonomous MindsStatuses as listed on trust.milo.ai, 19 November 2026

    1Overview

    Milo is built on a zero-trust architecture. Your data is never shared with third parties, and every request, including the AI model's, runs on our own servers.

    2Compliance and certifications

    GDPRCompliantEU customer data is processed only within the EU.
    ISO 27001CompliantCertificate available on the Trust Centre.
    SOC 2 Type ICompliantReport available on the Trust Centre.
    SOC 2 Type IIIn progressAudit under way.
    ICORegisteredMilo.ai (Autonomous Minds) is registered as a data controller with the UK ICO.

    3Security architecture

    • Zero trust by default. Every request is authenticated and authorised, and all traffic between services is encrypted and verified.
    • Regional data residency. Customer data stays in its designated region, to meet local regulatory and data sovereignty rules.
    • Enterprise controls. Audit trails, identity and access management, multi-factor authentication, role-based access and single sign-on.
    • Zero data retention. Customer data is never used to train models, and what is sent for an answer isn't kept once the answer is done.

    4External penetration test
