Where the week goes

Four problems, one shape.

Across 380 pubs and hotels there is no shortage of reporting. It sits in different departments, in different formats, in systems that were never asked to talk to each other, and every attempt to join them costs somebody a week.

Power BI

It grows until it has to be cut back

Reports get added, the model gets heavier, and at some point it has to be condensed again. That cycle is a permanent job for a team who would rather be building something new.

→ No model to maintain. Milo reads the sources.
Change requests

Every change is a few days away

A question in a trading meeting becomes a request, the request joins a queue behind month end, and by the time the report is right the decision has already been taken. The reports are good. The wait is what stops them being used.

→ Asked in the room. No build step, no queue.
The weekly pack

Five departments, five formats

Each head of department pulls their own numbers, builds their own graphs and drops them into their own deck. Finance splits a weekly deck across five or six people, a page each. Nothing reconciles, because nothing is joined.

→ One live pack that updates itself.
The blind spots

Sentiment and Collins sit outside

Guest sentiment lives in its own tool with its own AI. Collins is somewhere else again. Copilot reads one sales report and summarises that single level. None of them can put covers, labour, spend and what guests said into the same answer.

→ All of it, in one answer, with sources.
