Home truths

“Will the team use it?”

Usually two or three people start and use it all day. Others see what they've built and ask how. We speed that up: an in-person workshop in London, a shared Slack channel with our engineer, and the first alerts set up with you, so the signals arrive without anyone remembering to ask.

“Our data is messy.”

Milo works on it as it is. Where something needs explaining, tell it once in plain English, “a booking under seven days is a short let”, and it remembers. We work with national lotteries running billions of rows. Five million is comfortable.

“We already have Claude.”

Milo runs on models like Claude. The difference is everything around the model: live connections to your data, the same answer whoever asks, calculations run as queries on the full data rather than by the model, and permissions per person, so each team sees what it needs and nothing else.

“And Looker?”

Milo replaces it. Dashboards are built by asking for them, change when you ask, and every chart can be questioned: why did occupancy drop in Soho last month? One subscription instead of two.
