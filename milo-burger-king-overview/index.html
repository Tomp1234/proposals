    Getting started

    Start with one team.

    A pilot starts with one agile team, then the rest of the business joins in waves.

    STEP 1

    Connect

    Tills, the three platforms and the app for one area, read-only and on a sandbox first. Our engineers build it and onboard the team in person.

    STEP 2

    Teach

    We teach Milo your terms: like-for-likes, speed of service, delivery mix, your areas and your franchisees.

    STEP 3

    Ask

    The team asks in Teams, Slack or the browser, and pins what matters as dashboards and alerts.

    STEP 4

    Add teams

    Field, franchise and central teams join in waves. Users are unlimited, so nobody waits for a seat.
