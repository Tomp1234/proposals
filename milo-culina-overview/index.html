Why we put this together

In October, Culina brought ten businesses under one name, from Stobart, Great Bear and Fowler Welch to IPS, IRF and iForce. Customers now get the whole range through one partner.

The systems underneath came with each business. From your job adverts and supplier stories, finance and HR are moving onto SAP and much of the fleet runs Microlise, but warehousing and transport planning still differ from business to business. In a group built this way, a question across the businesses usually means exports and a spreadsheet.

Milo sits on top of all of it, alongside Boomi and Power BI, so anyone at Culina can ask across the group in plain English, from a shift manager to the board. Everything below runs on sample data, so click around.

    Getting started

    Start with one team.

    A pilot starts with one agile team, say the operations team at one chilled site, then the rest of Culina joins in waves.

    STEP 1

    Connect

    That site's warehouse, Microlise, SAP and workforce data, read-only and on a sandbox first. Our engineers build it and onboard the team in person.

    STEP 2

    Teach

    We teach Milo your terms: pallets and cases, on time in full, trailer fill, cost per drop and how each contract is charged.

    STEP 3

    Ask

    The team asks in Teams, Slack or the browser, and pins what matters as dashboards and alerts.

    STEP 4

    Add teams

    Transport, commercial, finance and people join in waves. Users are unlimited, so every site can have logins.
