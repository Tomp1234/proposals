What is the Milo Data Platform?

It's one place to connect, store, model and govern all of your business data, with Milo Analyst built in. Instead of running separate pipelines, a warehouse, a BI tool and a chatbot, you get one platform where anyone can ask a question and get a trusted answer.

Is Milo a data platform or an AI analyst?

Both. The platform handles syncing, storage, the semantic model and governance. Milo Analyst sits on top and does the analysis: answering questions, building dashboards and sending alerts. An analyst is only as good as the data under it, which is why we built both.

How is this different from ChatGPT or a BI tool?

ChatGPT can't see your live data. BI tools need someone to build every report. Milo stores and models your data, answers new questions as they come up, and alerts you when the numbers move.
