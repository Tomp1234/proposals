Dear Shaken Udder team,

Shaken Udder started in 2004 with a black truck and a cow on the side, making shakes at festivals. Twenty-one years on it's the UK's number one premium milkshake brand, with fibre and protein ranges, an ambient range for shelves without a fridge, and a new owner in Idilia.

Every route to the shopper reports in its own way: grocer portals, wholesaler depletions, Amazon, your own shop, the production plan and the ledger. A simple question, like whether last month's promotion paid for itself, means pulling four of them together by hand.

Milo connects them. Anyone at Shaken Udder asks a question in plain English and gets the answer in seconds, with its sources. A question can keep running as an alert, and the monthly pack can build itself.

Your campaign line is “do what makes you happy”, and for most people that isn't rebuilding a promo report on a Monday morning. The demo below starts at the marketing seat, with the fibre launch. Everything runs on sample data, so click around.
