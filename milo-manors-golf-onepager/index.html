    How it works

    Live in a week. Nothing for your team to build.

    1. Week oneWe connect it

      Shopify, Klaviyo, the ad accounts, the warehouse and Xero. Our engineers build and maintain every connection, including any without a ready-made connector.

    2. Every dayAnyone asks

      Marketing, ecommerce, ops or finance, in plain English. In Slack, WhatsApp or the browser, with no SQL and no exports.

    3. From then onIt runs itself

      Pin any answer as a live dashboard. Ask it every week and it becomes the 7am email, or an alert when a number moves.

    Trust centre

    Read-only, encrypted and certified.

    From the Milo Trust Centre, where the full reports and policies are available on request.

    Certified

    ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type I. SOC 2 Type II is in progress. GDPR.

    Hosted

    AWS in Frankfurt, across several availability zones, with database backups.

    Encrypted

    In transit and at rest, with managed production keys.

    Tested

    Penetration tests, code scanning, a web application firewall and intrusion detection.

    Separated

    Each customer's data is kept apart, and our access to production is least-privilege.

    Every connection reads and nothing is written back. Revoke the logins and the feeds stop.
