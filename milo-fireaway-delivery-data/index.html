What the delivery platforms actually give us

Deliveroo and Uber Eats, source by source.

Our engineers have been through both developer platforms in detail. This is what each one will and will not hand over, where the gaps are, and how we fill them. Written before any work starts so there are no surprises in week three.

Uber Eats gives us considerably more to work with. Real-time orders plus settled financial, operational and feedback reporting going back roughly six months.

Deliveroo gives us real-time orders and item-level refunds, but only 30 days of history and no payout, commission or ratings data through the API. That is a platform limitation, not a Milo one, and it is worth knowing now because it changes where some of your margin numbers have to come from.

Both cover only the stores you operate or that authorise us. Neither gives market or competitor data, and no tool built on these APIs can claim otherwise.

03

The gaps, and how we close them

Every gap has a route. None of them are blockers.
Gap

Deliveroo finance and ratings

Commission, payouts and ratings are not exposed by the API, so channel margin cannot come from Deliveroo alone.

How we close it

Restaurant Hub exports, joined to the order feed

We take finance from Hub exports and reconcile them against the live order data, so margin per store and per channel still resolves. If Deliveroo confirms an API route, we switch to it and nothing downstream changes.

Gap

Deliveroo history stops at 30 days

Anything older than a month is already gone and cannot be recovered later. Every day of delay is a day of history lost permanently.

How we close it

Start capturing webhooks now

We stand up the receiver early and run a daily Get Orders sweep as a safety net against missed pushes. The sooner this starts, the deeper your first year of trend data goes.

Gap

The platforms do not share item IDs

Deliveroo and Uber Eats each use their own identifiers, so the same pizza is a different product on each, and like-for-like comparison fails.

How we close it

Map both to your PLU and SKU

We map every platform item onto your own product codes, which is what makes cross-platform and POS comparison possible in a single query.

Gap

Live and settled numbers disagree

Uber Eats figures move for up to 72 hours as refunds and adjustments land, so a number read on the day will not match the invoice.

How we close it

Treat reports as the source of truth

Live orders drive the operational views, settled reports drive anything financial. Each number carries its source, so nobody presents a provisional figure to your board as final.

04

Getting access

Both need paperwork before code

Neither platform grants access on request alone. Both involve their partner teams, and the Uber Eats licence is the longer pole, so it is worth starting both in parallel on day one.

Deliveroo

Developer Portal, then their integration team
  • Register on the Developer Portal and complete OAuth
  • Work with their integration team to enable the webhooks
  • Refund webhook is switched on per brand, so it needs requesting explicitly
  • Restaurant Hub export access for the finance side

Uber Eats

Roughly 2 to 4 weeks for a reporting-only integration
  • NDA and API licence
  • Partner manager approval
  • eats.report and eats.order scopes
  • Reporting webhook endpoint registered and verified
