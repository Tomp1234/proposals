The question you got stuck on before

“Would we have to go through the whole thing again?”

When Business Central upgrades, table names change and move. You have been here before, in 2022, and called it pulling teeth. The fairest objection on the call, so here is a straight answer.

THE ANSWER

No. You do not rebuild it.

Connectors are monitored for schema drift (renamed tables, moved fields, changed types), and the change is detected rather than discovered by someone opening a broken report.

WHO DOES THE WORK

Our engineers, in days

What isn’t handled automatically is fixed by us, AI‑assisted, in a day or two rather than a month or two. It never becomes a line on your test plan or a change request with a partner.
