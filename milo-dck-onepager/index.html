Milo is an AI data analyst for the operations team. It connects to NetSuite, the DC and the sales and stock feeds from every host, then answers in plain English in seconds: which stores are short, where sell-through is slipping, and what the field team should do about it.

What it is

One analyst across every host feed, NetSuite and the DC at once, answering in the language the trading meeting uses.

What it replaces

The report queue. Host files stitched together in Excel, Power BI packs someone has to build first, answers that arrive on Thursday.

What it takes

Read-only access to what you already run. Live in a week, nothing to migrate, nothing new for the field team to learn.
