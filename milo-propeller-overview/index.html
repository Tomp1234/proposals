Dear Jody,

Propeller's own guide to PR and content for growth puts it plainly: “If you want to show that PR and content deliver ROI, you need to track it. Start with share of voice… Layer that with CRM data and track where leads come from.”

That's the hard part, because the proof sits in other people's systems. Coverage is in the monitoring tools, visits in each client's GA4 and leads in each client's CRM. Propeller's own numbers are split the same way, between London and New York, pounds and dollars, the CRM and the timesheets.

Milo joins them. Anyone at Propeller asks a question in plain English and gets the answer in seconds, with its sources. You already build single sources of truth in HubSpot for clients like Alkimi. Milo reads HubSpot and joins it to what HubSpot doesn't hold: finance, timesheets, coverage and each client's own data.

The closest example is Coolr, a London agency whose COO had her year-end board report from Milo in thirty minutes. The whole team uses it now. The demo below starts at the growth seat and runs on sample data, so click around.
