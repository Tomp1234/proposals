02 / Where your data lives

The data stays where it is.

Milo queries your data on your server. What crosses the boundary is a query and the result of that query, not the dataset.

Nothing else crosses the line.

What crosses

  • A query, written from the question somebody asked.
  • The result of that query, on its way back to the person who asked it.

What does not

  • A copy of your dataset. Nothing of yours is replicated into our systems.
  • Anything used to train a model, by us or by the model providers. That is written into the contract.

Milo reaches your data through an account you create and you control. You set what that account can see. You can narrow it, widen it or switch it off from your side at any point, and you do not need us to do it.

Switch it off and we hold nothing, because there was never a copy to delete.

Milo keeps the conversation: the questions your three people asked, and the answers they were shown, so they can go back to them. It does not build a copy of your systems on our side. When the contract ends, the conversation goes with it.

03 / How it is maintained

You maintain nothing.

No Milo software is installed on your server, so there is nothing on your side to patch, restart or keep a version of. No one at N.G.P. picks up a maintenance job because of it.

Updates and fixes happen on our infrastructure. New capability arrives without a release window and without your team scheduling anything around it.

The connection is the only standing thing between us, and it belongs to you. Beyond it we have no access to your environment. If the connection breaks, we tell you and we fix our end of it.

04 / Security and compliance

The evidence, and where to check it.

Certification
ISO 27001 compliant. SOC 2 Type I compliant, Type II in progress. Independent penetration testing, vulnerability scanning and an internal audit programme run alongside them.
Sub-processors
Five, all named publicly: Amazon Web Services for infrastructure, Cloudflare for edge security, ClickHouse for the warehouse, Fivetran for ingestion, Clerk for identity.
Access control
Roles are set by you and enforced at the data layer, so a person cannot read rows their role does not cover. Every question, and the query it produced, is logged with the user and the timestamp, and your team can export the log.
Retention
We hold no copy of your data, so there is no retention period on it to negotiate.
Where we work
Regulated gaming operators, and multi-site hospitality and retail businesses, where these questions get asked before anything is connected.
