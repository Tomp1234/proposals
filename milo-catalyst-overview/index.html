    Our guesses

    Where it probably pinches.

    Read from your website, so treat these as questions. Each one opens a seat in the demo below.

    Ads and results

    Tying each ad to what it sold

    Why it’s hardSpend sits in four ad platforms and TikTok Shop, sales in each client’s own tools. A £300k ad can fade between checks.

    What Milo doesTags every ad by hook, creator, format and angle, joins it to spend and sales, and flags fatigue the day it starts.

    Reporting

    Reports eat account managers’ weeks

    Why it’s hardDaily management, monthly reports and quarterly reviews across many clients, each wanting it shown their way.

    What Milo doesDrafts each client’s report in their own format from live numbers, ready to check rather than build.

    Capacity and margin

    Knowing which clients make money

    Why it’s hardUGC, motion and shoot days all cost differently, and revision rounds hide in the margin. When a client doubles spend, the asset count jumps too.

    What Milo doesMargin by client and format, and how many assets next month’s spend plans need from each team.

    Creators

    Fees, rights and affiliate payouts

    Why it’s hardCreator cost, delivery, performance and usage rights usually live in Airtable or Notion, updated by hand.

    What Milo doesJoins creator records to how their ads perform, and warns before rights expire on an ad that’s still spending.

    Retention

    Seeing a wobble before the client does

    Why it’s hardFalling spend, slipping results and slow sign-offs tend to surface once a client is already unhappy.

    What Milo doesA Monday list of accounts that need attention, and QBR packs that show your impact and where to grow.
