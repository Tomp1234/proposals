The 70% wall

Where other tools stopped.

You've seen tools reach 60 to 70% of what Peppermint needs. These are the four places they usually stop, and what Milo does at each.

Stopped at

Messy, unlinked data

Spreadsheets in every format, files that don't reference each other, columns that aren't plain English. Our engineers do the matching in week one, so your team doesn't spend the month cleaning data.

Stopped at

Systems nobody supports

There's no ready connector for a 15-year-old PHP platform. We build one, usually straight from its database and read-only. Building integrations from scratch is part of the service, for anything on the list of 30.

Stopped at

Integrations that break

Stability is where the vibe-coded build fell over. We monitor every connection and fix it when something changes, from a renamed column to a new export format. When the PHP platform is rebuilt, we move the connection across and the history carries on.

Stopped at

Nuance only Peppermint knows

A trading day that ends at 04:00, takings gross or net, which client is on which commercial model. You teach Milo once, in its company knowledge, and every answer, dashboard and alert uses it.

10 TB+

Our largest customer runs more than 10 TB, at millions of transactions an hour. 1.5 TB of SharePoint is well inside that.

Claude

You already use Claude across the company. It's good with words, but it can't see Peppermint's numbers until something joins the 30 systems and keeps them joined. That's the job Milo does.

The proof of concept

It's the off‑season. Let's start planning 2027 now.

One month on Peppermint's own data, with a customer success manager alongside you. You're hands-on from week two, and the pitch forecasts are ready for next season's pitches.

WEEK 1

Connect and teach

We connect SharePoint, the PHP platform and one season's EPOS, read-only, and write down Peppermint's terms with you: trading day, takings, spend per head, bar names.

WEEK 2

Hands-on

You, and two or three of the people who come to you for answers, ask your own questions. Anything Milo gets wrong goes back into the definitions.

WEEK 3

Pitch and automate

We build the pitch forecast from your past events, plus the dashboards and alerts you choose.

WEEK 4

Score it

We go through your requirements list together, line by line: what Milo covers, what it covers in part, and what it doesn't.
