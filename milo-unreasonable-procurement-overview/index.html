The shift

From price files and pivots to a question and an answer.

Procurement data is the least tidy data in hospitality. Price files arrive as spreadsheets, invoices arrive as PDFs, pack sizes never agree, and the client's EPOS knows nothing about either. None of that is broken, it was just never asked to line up.

How it usually works
  • Price files land as spreadsheets and get checked by hand, if at all
  • The saving is proved in a workbook that one person really understands
  • Price creep shows up on the invoice, weeks after the delivery
  • Pack sizes and units differ, so like for like is a manual exercise
  • Supplier volume gets reconciled at quarter end from whatever can be found
  • Every new client adds another version of the same monthly report
With Milo
  • Price files, invoices, credits, EPOS and the ledger read on one basis
  • Ask in plain English, answer in seconds with the invoice lines attached
  • An alert the day a line prices above the agreed rate, not at month end
  • Everything normalised to price per kilo, per litre, per unit
  • Volume by supplier is live, so profit share is a number rather than a job
  • Client statements build and send themselves, however many clients there are

How a pilot runs

One month, one client, real invoices.

Not a rollout. One client account and its supplier set, chosen because it proves the point fastest. The one with the most suppliers and the messiest price files is the right candidate, because the hard part is never volume, it is making files that were never designed to agree with each other agree.

Week 1

Connect

Credentials in, price files and invoice feeds connected, our engineers doing the building. Read only or sandbox access first if that is easier to sign off.

Week 2

Normalise it

Suppliers, products, pack sizes and units mapped so they compare properly. A 5kg case from one supplier and a 2.5kg case from another become the same price per kilo. This is where the questions become answerable at all.

Week 3

Real questions

You both ask it whatever you want. Price compliance, basket movement, supplier volume, category spend. Dashboards get pinned, the client statement gets scheduled.

Week 4

Go or no go

An honest review of what it answered well and what it did not. Nothing to unwind if the answer is no.

The downside of finding out is a month and one client's supplier set, with nothing to unwind if the answer is no. Commercials are straightforward and we will put them in front of you whenever you want them, but they are not the interesting part of this conversation yet.
