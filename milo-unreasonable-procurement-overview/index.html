What you asked

Your questions, answered properly.

The ones that came up on the call, with the answers written down this time.

Why not just connect our clients to Claude?

For one person with a couple of spreadsheets and HubSpot, Claude is great and we would tell you to use it. Past that, three things change. Milo ingests the data rather than reading it when you ask, so years of invoices are there and answers come back fast. Connectors to bespoke systems like P2P and EPOS tend to break within days, and our engineers keep them running. And two people on Claude get two different answers, whereas in Milo the team shares one knowledge base, with controls on who sees what.

Can it read PDF invoices, and cope with catchweight?

Yes. PDFs, CSVs, spreadsheets and system exports. Lines match on product code, so a description that changes month to month is still the same product. Units, pack sizes and catchweight are taught once in the knowledge base. For one pub client, a day means 12 till 4. Once Milo was told that, it got it right every time after.

Does the monthly fee limit how many systems we connect?

No. Connect as many as you need. Price follows usage, counted in interactions. Your founding price includes 200 a month, plenty for the two of you, and a team of around twenty typically uses about 500. Once the dashboards and routines are built you ask less, because the answers come to you. We also pick the right model for each question, so a price check takes seconds rather than minutes.

Can it bring in data from outside the business?

Yes. Market prices, weather, holidays and trends. For a Nisa Local store it gathered Coca-Cola prices from nearby shops to show how far price could move and stay competitive. For you, that is the potato price index: every client's prices set against the market and against each other.

What should we do now so we are ready?

Keep your data up to date, in whatever form it is in. Excel and CSV exports are a fine start. A high-end London restaurant group we signed recently started from CSV exports of their booking system and HubSpot, before a single integration was built.

Could our clients use Milo too?

Yes, in two ways. Refer a client and you earn 10% of their first-year contract. Further on, Milo can run under your name as the data platform behind all your clients, each seeing only their own data. That second route is the bigger opportunity, and we would price it with you once the pilot has proved the concept.

Before it touches a single supplier file

Negotiated rates are the most sensitive data you hold.

Milo is SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified and independently penetration tested. Access is role based down to the individual, and each client's data is kept separate, so no client sees another's pricing and no supplier sees the terms behind the next. An NDA goes in place before we connect anything.
