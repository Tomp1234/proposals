Dear Punch team,

Punch describes its model as data, infrastructure and economies of scale, combined with the entrepreneurial flair of independent publicans. This page is about the data part, across both halves of the estate.

In Pub Partnerships, Punch supplies the stock, the menus and the EPOS, so every sale is already Punch data. Your latest results could say those pubs were up 59% on England's World Cup match days. The same figure for the leased and tenanted estate sits in about 900 tills that belong to the publicans. What Punch sees there is rent, tied purchases, visit notes, repairs and lease dates, each in its own system.

Milo connects both sides. An operations manager, a BDM, a partner or the board asks a question in plain English and gets the answer in seconds, with its sources. Milo also works the other way round: every Monday, each person gets a short list of recommendations, with the numbers behind each one.

Below are the two models, a seat for each role, the Monday recommendations, the systems Milo connects, and pub and bar groups already using it. Everything runs on sample data, so click around.

Two models

Two models, two kinds of data.

The partnership estate runs on Punch's own tills. The tenanted estate runs on what each publican buys and pays. Milo works with both, and with the pubs moving from one to the other.

Pub Partnerships

31% · about 400 pubs
Who runs it
A partner on a five-year agreement, paid an uncapped share of turnover. Punch supplies the stock, menus, EPOS and apps, across Fireside, Our Local and Unity.
What Punch sees
Every sale, line by line, because the tills are Punch's. Stock, GP, rotas and bookings too.
Who asks
Operations managers, business support advisors, partners and the heads of each format.
Milo's first job
Turn the till data Punch already has into answers, and a weekly list of what to do, for every partner and operations manager.

Leased & Tenanted

69% · about 900 pubs
Who runs it
An independent publican on a tenancy or lease, paying rent and, where tied, buying their beer through Punch.
What Punch sees
Rent, tied purchases, BDM visit notes, repairs and lease dates, each in its own system. The till is the publican's.
Who asks
BDMs, operations directors and the property team.
Milo's first job
Put rent, tie, visits and repairs side by side, so every BDM knows which pub needs them first, and which could move to Pub Partnerships.
