A morning at Advance

Same morning. Two very different versions of it.

Five moments from the routine across Corby, Sheffield, Bradford and Germany: how each runs today, against one month into the proof of concept.

The Flash

Today · from 06:00

Each site pulls yesterday's costs and revenue, builds its own version, then feeds a master. Roughly ten hours of work across the network before the business knows whether yesterday made money.

With Milo · 06:30

Already in inboxes. Compiled across all four sites overnight, rolled into the master view, with the why behind every variance attached, not just the number.

Daily customer reports

Today · 08:45 onwards

Every account manager downloads stock holding, picked, packed and despatched, into a layout, XLOOKUPs across tabs, Power BI snips pasted into an email. 30 to 45 minutes each, every morning. If a refresh fails, someone goes digging.

With Milo · drafted by 09:30

One per site, one per customer, sitting in each account manager's drafts folder. They give it the once-over and press send. As confidence grows, even that step can go.

Invoicing

Today · Monday morning

Each account manager spends the morning building invoices across 60 to 80 regular customers, merging WMS extracts with transport invoices, spreadsheet upon spreadsheet.

With Milo · Monday 08:00

Drafts ready, exceptions flagged. Milo has merged the WMS and transport data and drafted the run. The handful that need a human eye are marked. The rest are a click.

An ad-hoc question

Today · whenever it lands

A customer emails complaining about late deliveries. Someone finds the person who owns that report, waits for a Power BI refresh, and stitches the answer together by hand.

With Milo · same minute

Anyone asks Milo directly: every delivery this year, on time versus late, with reasons. No refresh queue, no XLOOKUPs, no waiting for the one person who knows the report.

Someone is on leave

Today

The SOP folder comes out. Someone else inherits the spreadsheet, finds the tabs, and hopes the XLOOKUPs hold.

With Milo

Nothing changes. The reports never depended on them being in.

Beyond reporting

Milo acts on the data, not just reports it.

Anything can summarise a spreadsheet. Milo watches your data continuously and then does something about it: drafts the email, logs the ticket, updates the tool. Because your WMS already speaks APIs and webhooks, Milo can action inside the warehouse system and everywhere around it.

When a shipment despatches

The manifest sends itself

Milo compiles the manifest and sends it to the distribution list the moment the WMS confirms despatch. No one builds it, no one forgets it.

→ Zero-touch, end to end
When a customer's volumes dip

The account manager knows first

Milo spots the trend across orders and invoicing, emails the account manager with the picture, and logs a ticket in your in-house help desk. The human makes the call.

→ Drafted + ticketed, human sends
When a fault complaint lands

The stock goes on hold

Milo places the product line on hold via the WMS API, issues checks to stock control, and emails the right people, the exact workflow you described, without being locked inside one system.

→ Actioned in the WMS + beyond
