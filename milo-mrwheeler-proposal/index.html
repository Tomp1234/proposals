What Milo is

Ask the business a question. Get the answer while it still matters.

Milo is an AI data analyst. It connects to the systems Mr.Wheeler already runs and answers questions about trading, stock, customers and margin in plain English, in seconds. There is no dashboard to specify first, no export into a spreadsheet, and no waiting on the one person who knows where the numbers live.

What it is

An analyst that has already read everything. It holds your orders, stock, customers and spend at the same time, so one question can cross all of them.

What it replaces

The export and the spreadsheet, and the report that has to be built by somebody before it can tell you anything at all.

What it takes

Read only access to the systems you have. Live in about a week, nothing to migrate, and nothing new for the team to learn.

How it works

Connect it once, then just ask.

01

Connect

Milo reads from what you already run, over 700 integrations plus your own database and the spreadsheets people actually use. Read only. Nothing moves, nothing is rebuilt.

02

Ask

In the browser, in Slack or in Teams, in plain English. Milo works out which data the question needs, runs the numbers across every system at once, and shows its working.

03

Act

Every answer comes with the reason behind it and an offer to do the next thing: pull the list, draft the note, or send the digest at eight in the morning before the trading meeting.

Milo never asks a language model to do arithmetic. Calculations run against your data, so the figures are right every time and each one traces back to the row it came from. The dashboards then build themselves out of whatever the team asks most.
