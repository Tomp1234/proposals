How it works

Ask. Pin. Automate.

Three steps, and each one feeds the next. A question becomes a dashboard, and the dashboard becomes something that sends itself.

01 · Ask

Plain English in, answer out

Typed the way you'd say it across the desk. The answer comes back in seconds with the rows behind it, so it can be checked rather than taken on trust.

“Rate of sale by flavour in Tesco this week, against the four-week average.”

02 · Pin

Dashboards built by anyone

Pin any answer and it becomes a live view that refreshes itself. National account managers build their own retailer views and ecommerce builds the subscriber view, with no data team and no ticket.

At Two Tribes, one rep built her own account book without being asked.

03 · Automate

A question becomes a routine

Anything worth asking every Monday gets scheduled. The trading pack writes and sends itself, and alerts fire the day a store stops selling or a venue stops ordering.

Delivered to email, Slack, Teams or WhatsApp.

An internal build is really two projects: the integrations underneath, and the tool people actually use on top. We build the integrations from scratch for you, and the tool already exists.

How a pilot runs

Four weeks. One team.
Your real numbers.

Two Tribes' advice after their first months was to start with one defined job and a few internal champions, then let it spread. So a pilot picks one team. Ecommerce is the quickest start, since store data is clean and the questions come fast. The supermarket side is the biggest prize.

  1. Week 1

    Connect

    Credentials in, and our engineers do the building. Read only or sandbox access first if that's easier to sign off.

  2. Week 2

    Teach it Au

    Flavours, formats and pack sizes mapped so every channel reads in the same units, down to nine-litre cases. House rules go in once.

  3. Week 3

    Real questions

    Your team asks it whatever they want. Dashboards get pinned and the Monday trading pack gets scheduled.

  4. Week 4

    Go or no go

    An honest review of what it answered well and what it didn't. Nothing to unwind if the answer is no.
