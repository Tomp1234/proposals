How it connects

Milo reads and never writes back. Data is loaded in full, not skimmed from an export, so every figure is calculated from your own records.

For finance and IT

Security

ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type 2 and GDPR. Hosted in Frankfurt. Your data is never used to train AI, never sold or shared. Access is by role, so a plant manager sees their plant. Documents at trust.milo.ai.

Accuracy

The AI never does the sums. Calculations run on your data and every figure shows its source. Your definitions, such as what counts as on time, are written down once, so everyone who asks gets the same answer.

Where it shows up

Live plant and finance dashboards built in Milo, a 06:00 briefing to each plant manager, and alerts on Teams or email when a plant falls behind or a cost runs over.
