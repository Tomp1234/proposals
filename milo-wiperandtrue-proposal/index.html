04 / The overlap question

Shopify has AI. Xero has AI. Neither answers the question you have.

The AI going into the systems you run is good and getting better. It is also locked inside one box, because that is where the product ends.

01

Shopify's assistant knows the webshop and nothing about a batch

It can tell you what sold and to whom. It cannot tell you what that beer cost to make, because brew costs live in Brewman and it has never heard of Brewman.

02

Xero knows what you were paid, not what you brewed

Invoice ageing is there. Whether the account paying slowly is also ordering less is not, because that sits in another system under another name for the same customer.

03

Every question worth asking at your size crosses two systems

Margin per hectolitre. Which accounts are drifting. What to brew next. None of those sit in one product, which is why they get answered by someone building a spreadsheet. The joining-up is the work, and none of them will do it.

Not everyone sees everything

Xero holds payroll. Roles are set per person and per source, so venue teams get trading and stock and salary lines stay where they are. Full log of who asked what, and your data is never used to train a model.

It can act without being asked

Two invoices running late and orders falling in the same month goes to the account manager that morning, not at month end. Written in plain English, delivered by email, Slack or Teams.

Everyone gets a seat

Fifty-odd people and nobody has to learn what a data model is. Priced flat because it only works once the questions stop coming through one person.
